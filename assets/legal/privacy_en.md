# Privacy Policy
Version 1.0 · Effective 16 November 2026

## 1. Who is responsible
The restaurant that runs this service (the "Restaurant") decides how your data is used. The Foodz software only processes it on the Restaurant's behalf. You can reach the Restaurant in Settings → Restaurant contact.

## 2. What we collect and why
- Account: your email address, name, phone number and chosen language. Used to sign you in, to call you about orders and to show the app in your language.
- Orders: the dishes, totals, delivery area, note, order code, status and times. Used to prepare, deliver and keep a record of your order.
- Order contact details: your name, phone number, delivery address and, if you choose to add it, a map position. Only you, the Restaurant's admins and, once they take your order, the delivery person can see them. The kitchen cannot.
- Consent record: which version of these documents you accepted, and when.
- Notification token: a code that lets your phone or browser receive order alerts. It is removed when you sign out.
We do not collect payment card data, your contacts, your photos or your location in the background. We use no advertising and no analytics.

## 3. Who else handles data
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database and Cloud Messaging) stores accounts and orders and sends notifications. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google also signs you in.
- Supabase stores dish pictures only, never personal data.
- If you pick a position on a map, your device loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap servers, which can see your IP address.
Your data may be processed on servers outside Algeria.

## 4. How long we keep it
- Profile and notification tokens: until you delete your account.
- Orders: as long as the Restaurant needs them for accounting and disputes. When you delete your account, the contact details of your orders are deleted too.

## 5. Your rights
In Settings you can edit your details, export your data and delete your account. You can also ask the Restaurant to correct or delete data, or to stop using it, in line with Algerian Law 18-07 on the protection of personal data.

## 6. Security
Access is limited by role and enforced by the database rules. Data travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never seen by the Restaurant. No system is perfectly secure: tell the Restaurant if you notice a problem.

## 7. Children
The app is for people aged 16 and over.

## 8. Changes
When this policy changes, the app asks you to accept the new version.
