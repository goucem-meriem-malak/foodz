# Terms of Use
Version 1.0 · Effective 16 November 2026

## 1. Who we are
Foodz is the ordering app used by the restaurant that runs this service (the "Restaurant"). The Restaurant prepares and delivers your orders. Its contact details are in Settings → Restaurant contact.

## 2. Using the app
- You must be at least 16 years old, or order together with a parent or guardian.
- Give accurate information: your name, a phone number that works and a correct delivery address.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

## 3. How an order works
- Placing an order is a request, not a final contract. The Restaurant calls you on the number you gave to confirm it. The order only starts once it is confirmed.
- The Restaurant may refuse or cancel an order, for example when a dish is unavailable or the address is out of range. It will tell you why.
- You can cancel an order yourself only until the Restaurant confirms it. After that, call the Restaurant.
- You can have only one unconfirmed order at a time.

## 4. Prices and payment
- Prices are in Algerian dinars (DZD). The delivery fee set by the Restaurant is shown before you order.
- There is no online payment. You pay in cash to the delivery person when your order arrives.
- The app never asks for your bank card.

## 5. Delivery
Preparation and delivery times shown in the app are estimates, not promises. Please be reachable by phone and at the address.

## 6. Acceptable use
- Do not place false or prank orders, and do not use someone else's details.
- Do not try to reach other people's data, break the security of the app or overload it.
The Restaurant can suspend an account that breaks these rules.

## 7. Your account
You can edit your details, export your data or delete your account at any time in Settings. The Restaurant may suspend or close an account that is misused.
